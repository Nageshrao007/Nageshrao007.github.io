Terms of Service — Fact Arcade Studio

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Fact Arcade Studio ("the app") is a private publishing tool operated by the creator of the Fact Arcade channels. It is used only to publish Fact Arcade's own original videos to Fact Arcade's own social media accounts.

Who can use it

The app is not offered to the public. Only the owner of the Fact Arcade accounts signs in and uses it.

What the app does

With the account owner's permission (granted through TikTok Login Kit), the app uploads finished videos and their captions to the owner's own TikTok account using the TikTok Content Posting API. It does not post to anyone else's account.

Content

All videos are original works produced by Fact Arcade using our own animation and free, properly licensed images and sounds. We follow TikTok's Community Guidelines and Terms of Service.
