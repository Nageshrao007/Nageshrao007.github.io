Privacy Policy — Fact Arcade Studio

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Fact Arcade Studio is a private tool used only by the owner of the Fact Arcade accounts to publish their own videos. This policy explains what it handles.

Information we access

  • TikTok: the basic profile of the signed-in account (display name), used only to show which account is connected, and permission to upload and publish videos to that same account.
  • Access tokens: stored only on the owner's own computer, never shared or sold, and deleted when the account is disconnected.

What we don't do

  • We do not collect information about viewers or other TikTok users.
  • We do not sell, rent or share any data with third parties.
  • We do not use data for advertising.

Removing access

The account owner can disconnect the app at any time in the app or in TikTok's settings (Security → Manage app permissions), which revokes its access.
